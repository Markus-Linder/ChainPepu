Pre-Fab Review

A respin costs a week and four figures. A review costs a fraction of that. Send the KiCad project, get a prioritized findings report within 3 business days: what blocks the order, what to fix before the next rev, what is fine.

Request a review

You'll get a short intake form (board type, fab, quantity, what worries you) and a quote within one business day. Files are deleted 30 days after delivery unless you ask otherwise.

What you get

1. Tool-chain pass

DRC and ERC with every severity enabled, project rules and net classes compared against your fab's capability profile, BOM checked for missing part numbers, stock and basic/extended status, Gerber layers rendered and inspected.

2. Engineer pass

A human reads the schematic and layout against the 37-item checklist: decoupling, resets and straps, protection, return paths, signal groups, thermal, DFM, silkscreen, outputs. Not a rubber stamp.

3. Report

Findings ranked: blockers, should-fix, nice-to-have. Each with location, evidence and a proposed fix. Plus an agent prompt so your Claude Code or Codex session can apply the mechanical fixes under your supervision.
