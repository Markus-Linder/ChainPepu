2. Hosting and server logs

ser7 is hosted by Higgsfield on the Cloudflare network. When you open the site, technically necessary data is processed (IP address, time, requested page, browser type). Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (secure and stable operation). Cloudflare, Inc. (USA) is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. [Check: data processing agreement with Higgsfield/Cloudflare]

3. Search

Your search terms are sent from our server to the Wikimedia Foundation (Wikipedia, Wikimedia Commons, USA) to fetch results. Your IP address is not passed on for this. Search terms are not stored by us.

4. Content in the 3D street

  • Websites near you are loaded directly from their operators in embedded frames. Your browser then connects to these websites, which receive your IP address and may set their own cookies. Videos are loaded from Wikimedia Commons or, for submitted videos, from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com).
  • The 3D engine (three.js) is loaded from the jsDelivr CDN; your IP address is transmitted to it.
  • Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (displaying the search results you requested).

5. Submissions

When you submit a website or video, we store the details you enter (address, title, description, keywords, offer, optional email). We fetch the submitted page once for the automated content check. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR. Rejected entries are deleted regularly, at the latest after 90 days.

6. Sponsored placements and payment

For bookings we store the ad details, your email address and the booking status. Payment is processed by Stripe Payments Europe Ltd., Dublin, Ireland; payment data is entered directly with Stripe and does not reach us. Stripe may transfer data to the USA. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) and (c) GDPR (contract, retention duties under tax law: up to 10 years). We count impressions and visits of ads without identifying individual users.

7. Cookies and local storage

ser7 itself does not use tracking or advertising cookies. The admin area stores its sign-in only for the current browser session (sessionStorage).

8. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR), and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Contact us at the address above.
